Our services

Engineering-driven survey solutions

End-to-end surveying support for every phase of your development journey.

Topographical Survey

Detailed land profiling with contour, slope and structural data for planning and engineering.

  • Elevation & contour mapping
  • Terrain analysis
  • Design-ready output

Boundary Survey

Precise boundary determination and demarcation to strengthen legal and project certainty.

  • Property line verification
  • Dispute prevention
  • Fencing & layout support

Building Marking

Exact setting out for foundations, grids and structural alignment with construction precision.

  • Grid & column marking
  • Level transfer
  • Reduced rework

Contour Survey

Elevation and slope analysis for drainage planning, earthwork and site optimization.

  • Drainage planning
  • Earthwork estimation
  • Feasibility analysis

DGPS Survey

High-accuracy satellite measurement for large-scale infrastructure and engineering works.

  • Precise coordinate data
  • Large-area coverage
  • Reliable base points

As-Built Survey

Final documentation to verify completed construction against approved plans and site conditions.

  • Final validation
  • Compliance checks
  • Documentation support
