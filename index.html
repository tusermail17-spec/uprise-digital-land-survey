What we do

Surveying services for every stage

From first measurements to final verification, get dependable information for your next project.

Topographical survey

Detailed mapping of levels, contours, natural features and built elements for planning and design.

Boundary survey

Accurate boundary identification and demarcation to support ownership, fencing and development.

Building marking

Precise setting out of foundations, grids, columns and levels to keep construction on plan.

Contour survey

Elevation and slope data for drainage, earthwork estimation and efficient site planning.

DGPS survey

High-accuracy satellite positioning for large sites, infrastructure and engineering work.

As-built survey

Final documentation that verifies completed work against approved drawings and site conditions.
