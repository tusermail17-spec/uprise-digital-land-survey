Our services

Complete surveying support at every project stage

We provide dependable data for land assessment, planning, documentation and execution.

Topographical Survey

Mapping of levels, contours, slopes and site features for planning and engineering design.

  • Digital land profile
  • Contour & elevation data
  • Design-ready survey layouts

Boundary Survey

Precise property line identification and demarcation for legal and practical use.

  • Boundary demarcation
  • Dispute prevention support
  • Land registration clarity

Building Marking

Exact setting out for foundations, grids and structural alignment with construction precision.

  • Column & grid marking
  • Level transfer
  • Reduced rework

Contour Survey

Elevation and slope analysis for drainage planning, grading and risk reduction.

  • Drainage planning
  • Earthwork estimation
  • Construction feasibility

DGPS Survey

High-accuracy positioning for large-scale sites, infrastructure and engineering projects.

  • High-precision coordinate data
  • Large-area coverage
  • Reliable engineering base data

As-Built Survey

Final documentation to verify completed construction against approved plans and site conditions.

  • Final site validation
  • Documentation support
  • Regulatory compliance
